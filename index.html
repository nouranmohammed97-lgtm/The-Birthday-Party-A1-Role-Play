📖 Lesson 2.4: Here’s my band

Prepositions of place: in (in Seattle) · between (between Nuwa and me) · next to (next to me) · on the left / on the right

Describing people: boring, cool, friendly, funny, interesting, shy, smart

Short forms: Here’s = Here is · She’s = She is · It’s = It is · I’m = I am · What’s = What is · When’s = When is

Writing with “and”: connect words (interesting and smart) or two sentences (We’re four college students in Seattle, and we’re in a band.)

Accuracy: after a preposition use me, not I: Guy is next to me ✓

📖 Lesson 2.5: True for me

Discuss: Hello. / Hi. · My name is … · True for me. · Not true for me. · Really? (for surprise) · Goodbye.

Present: (Name) is the same. · (Name) is different.

Prepare: My mom is (nationality). My dad is (age). My grandmother is (name). My grandfather is from (city). My best friend is (personality). My birthday is in (month).
